Transport

Getting Around Cebu

IT Park Terminal

The IT Park Terminal is located within Cebu IT Park and provides access to public transportation going to different areas around Cebu.

It's a convenient option if you prefer public transportation instead of booking a private ride.

Grab & Taxis

Grab is one of the easiest and most convenient ways to get around Cebu. Taxis are also available around IT Park.

When booking a ride, use Avida Towers Riala as your pickup or drop-off location and follow the designated pickup area.

Walking Around IT Park

Many restaurants, cafés, convenience stores, shops, and entertainment spots are within walking distance from the unit.

Sugbo Mercado and Ayala Central Bloc are just a short walk away.

Airport & Long-Distance Trips

For trips to Mactan-Cebu International Airport or destinations outside IT Park, Grab or taxi is usually the most convenient option.

Travel time may vary depending on traffic, so allow extra time when heading to the airport or catching a scheduled trip.

Activities

Things to Do Nearby

  • 🏀 Magnum Sports Complex

    Play sports, work out, or enjoy an active day close to IT Park.

  • 🍢 Sugbo Mercado

    Explore Cebu's popular food market and try a variety of local and international dishes.

  • 🛍️ Ayala Central Bloc

    Shop, dine, grab coffee, watch a movie, or simply explore the mall.

  • ☕ Explore IT Park

    Walk around the area and discover cafés, restaurants, shops, and nightlife spots.

Cebu City Adventures

  • 🏛️ Cebu Heritage & City Tour

    Visit Magellan's Cross, Basilica Minore del Santo Niño, Fort San Pedro, and other historic landmarks.

  • 🌆 Cebu Highlands

    Visit Temple of Leah, Sirao Garden, and Tops for mountain views, photos, and a cooler escape from the city.

  • 🐠 Cebu Ocean Park

    A great option for families and anyone interested in marine life.

  • 🍖 Food Trip

    Try Cebuano favorites such as lechon, seafood, local desserts, and other Filipino dishes around the city.

Adventure Outside Cebu City

  • 🌊 Moalboal

    Go snorkeling, see the famous sardine run, and look for sea turtles.

  • 💦 Kawasan Falls

    Enjoy the turquoise waterfalls or try canyoneering for a more adventurous experience.

  • 🐋 Oslob

    Explore the southern coast and visit the area known for whale shark encounters and nearby attractions.

  • 🏔️ Osmeña Peak

    Hike to Cebu's highest point and enjoy panoramic mountain views.

  • 🏝️ Island & Beach Trips

    Spend the day swimming, snorkeling, diving, or exploring Cebu's nearby islands and beaches. Bantayan Island, Crab Island, Olango Island, and many more

Need Help Planning?

Cebu's major attractions are spread across the island. For longer trips such as Moalboal, Kawasan Falls, Oslob, or Osmeña Peak, plan your travel time in advance.

Ask us for recommendations based on your available time, interests, and preferred type of activity.
